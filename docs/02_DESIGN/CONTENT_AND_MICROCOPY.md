## 1. Voice & Patient Microcopy Principles

1. **Warm, Respectful, One Question at a Time:** Avoid medical jargon and interrogation tone; acknowledge answers gently (*"Thank you for sharing that with me"*).
2. **Strict Non-Diagnostic Boundary:** Companio never implies it is a doctor, diagnoses illness, or advises altering medications.
3. **Honest Concern Routing Copy (`CheckInComplete.jsx`):** Never reassure falsely! Display three strictly distinct stages backed by real database evidence:
   - **"Recorded for your care team"** (Flag/Concern created)
   - **"Notification sent"** *(only shown when delivery evidence exists on `EscalationEvent`)*
   - **"Reviewed by your care team"** *(only shown when clinician acknowledgement evidence exists on `Flag`)*
4. **Neutral Clinical Displays:**
   - **Labs (`PatientPathology.jsx`):** Show source reference ranges neutrally with explicit notice: *"Your clinician will explain your results."*
   - **Medications (`PatientMedications.jsx`):** Separate prescribed schedule from patient-reported logs; zero dosage advice or edit buttons.
   - **Missing Data:** Label as `Not recorded`, `Declined`, or `Needs clarification` — never `0`.
5. **Emergency Services Framing:** Use organization-configured emergency numbers (with documented default) paired with explicit text: *"Call emergency services (<number>)"* instead of hardcoded UI `911` assumptions.
6. **Consent Withdrawal Consequences:** Each of the 5 consent toggles explains exact functional consequences before confirmation (e.g., revoking `LIVE_TRANSCRIPTION` aborts active voice check-in sessions).

## 2. Zero-PHI Outbound Alert Copy

External SMS/Email notices use non-identifying template copy generated by `createZeroPhiNotificationPayload()` (`deidentificationService.js`):
> *"Notice: High-priority clinical alert #ALERT_REF_XXXXXX awaiting review. Patient details protected. Authenticate to review: https://app.companio.health/clinician/triage"*
