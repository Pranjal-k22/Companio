# Product Scope and Limitations

**Owner category:** Product and Clinical Governance  
**Status:** Current scope statement  
**Last verified:** 2026-11-12

## In scope

- Voice-first daily and weekly wellness check-ins for adults aged 55+.
- Capture and validation of structured patient-reported observations.
- Medication adherence and side-effect reporting.
- Symptom, sleep, hydration, nutrition, activity, mood and wellbeing check-ins.
- Deterministic safety screening against a predefined RED/YELLOW taxonomy.
- Clinician-facing triage, longitudinal review and draft report workflows.
- Explicit patient-controlled caregiver delegation with granular permissions.
- Auditable escalation and background notification processing.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Autonomous diagnosis.
- Prescribing, medication or treatment-plan changes.
- Replacing clinical judgment.
- Emergency dispatch or guaranteed emergency response.
- Treating caregiver relationship or emergency-contact status as automatic authorization.
- Automatically approving or publishing clinical reports.

## Deployment limitations

Provider and production-readiness records identify contractual and external review prerequisites. Keep synthetic-data mode as the default until intended providers, data flows and deployment jurisdictions have been approved.

Human research recruitment requires written ethics determination and an approved protocol. Controlled clinical pilot and commercial deployment have separate gates.

## Product claims

Do not market Companio as HIPAA certified, HIPAA Safe Harbor de-identified, an autonomous diagnostic system or a replacement for medical care. Claims require current legal, regulatory and clinical evidence.
